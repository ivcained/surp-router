05 / 10
The moat

The cache auction.

A repeated question costs almost nothing to serve. So surp prices it at 0.1 cents, runs the cache as a second-price auction, and pays the people whose cached answers get reused. The more popular a question, the cheaper it gets for everyone. Everyone else gets slower and pricier at scale. Ours inverts.
